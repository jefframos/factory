    Wheel: zoom · Right/middle drag or Space+drag: pan
    Delete: remove selection · Esc: cancel tool
    Wall tool: click points, Enter/double-click to finish, Esc to cancel.
    Door/Window: click on a wall. Pick a model, then click the map to drop it.
    Checkpoints are visited in order; the last one is the exit. Reveal at N hides an item until checkpoint N is reached (0 = visible from the start).
