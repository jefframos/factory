# Dig & Discover — store listing kit

## Title
**Dig & Discover**

## Short description (≤ 80 characters)
Dig the mine, sell your loot, unearth lost treasures and build your dream hub!

## Long description
Grab your shovel and head into the mine! In **Dig & Discover** every block you break is loot: dirt and stone, glittering copper, silver, gold and crystal. Fill your backpack, haul it back to the ATM and cash in.

Spend your earnings on better gear and watch your digging power grow, from a humble shovel to pickaxes, power drills and the legendary **Tractor**, which you ride through the tunnels at full speed.

The deeper you dig, the stranger the rock gets: five zones, from soft topsoil down to the Obsidian Depths. Hidden in the rock are **18 lost artifacts**, from an old bone to the legendary Lost Vault. Every artifact you find goes on display in your trophy room.

Back at the hub there's plenty to do:
- **Build your hub**: buy couches, lamps, plants, shelves and more, and watch the place come alive one corner at a time.
- **Dress up**: 36 faces, 18 colors and 18 hats in the wardrobe.
- **Complete achievements**: dig, collect and earn your way to cash rewards and special trophies for your hub.

Relaxing, satisfying and endlessly rewarding: how deep will you go?

## Controls
- **Mobile**: drag anywhere to move. You dig automatically when you walk into rock.
- **Desktop**: WASD / arrow keys or click-and-drag to move.

## Key features (bullets for store pages)
- Satisfying voxel digging: chunky blocks, flying chips, loot flying into your bag
- 7 tools to unlock, ending with a rideable tractor
- 5 mine zones and 18 hidden artifacts to collect
- Build and decorate your own hub, section by section
- 36 faces, 18 colors and 18 hats to mix and match
- Achievements with cash rewards and hub trophies
- 24 languages, mobile- and desktop-friendly, saves automatically

## Icon brief

Store icons are seen tiny (64–128 px) next to hundreds of others, so the icon has to read in a split second.

**Subject**: the miner, big and close: the chunky blue body with the cube head, the **Brave** grin face and the **yellow mining helmet** with its lamp. Show them mid-swing with a pickaxe, or bursting out of a broken block. Their face should fill about 40–50% of the icon.

**The hook**: a block cracking open with a gold nugget or glowing crystal popping out, plus a few flying chips. That says "dig → loot" without any words. An optional gold coin or treasure-chest glint adds the reward.

**Composition**
- One clear focal point (the miner's face and helmet) and one secondary (the loot burst). Nothing else.
- A slight low angle, with the character looking towards the viewer.
- A tight crop: let the helmet and pickaxe break the frame edge, for energy.
- No small details. Drop the hub, the furniture and the UI.

**Color**
- A warm, saturated background so it pops in a grid of icons: a sunny orange-to-yellow radial glow (the mine's dirt tones, brightened), or a sky blue that sets off the yellow helmet.
- Gold and crystal-cyan accents on the loot.
- A thick dark outline around the character (the game's UI style: chunky, cartoony, clean edges).

**Do / don't**
- Do: test it at 64 px (still readable?), test it on white and dark store backgrounds, and keep the same character as in the game (players trust icons that match the gameplay).
- Don't: add text (platforms show the title separately, and text won't survive 64 px), use thin lines, or crowd in several characters.

**Sizes to export**: 512×512 master (rounded corners are added by most platforms; keep 8% safe margins), plus 1024×1024 if required. Some platforms (Poki, CrazyGames) also take a wide 16:9 or 2:1 cover. For that, put the miner and loot burst on the left third and a slice of the finished hub (couches, trophy tables) on the right.

**Reference shots in this folder**: `player-*.png` show the miner's look and outfits up close, and `player-tractor.png` shows the tractor (good for a "level up" variant of the icon or cover art).
