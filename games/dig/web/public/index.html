    Shortcuts & help

    Wheel: zoom · Right/middle drag or Space+drag: pan
    ⌘/Ctrl+S: save · Delete: remove · R: rotate · Arrows: nudge · Esc: cancel
    Wall tool: click points, Enter/double-click to finish.
    Door/Window: click on a wall.
    Checkpoints are visited in order; the last one is the exit. Reveal at N hides an item until checkpoint N (0 = always visible).
