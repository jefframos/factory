Gameplay Feedback & Improvements
Progression & Economy
- Add significantly more bag upgrade levels. I completed the bag upgrades in the first area very quickly.
- Review and increase upgrade prices to create a better sense of progression and escalation.
- Make prices scale more aggressively as the player progresses through the areas.
- Simplify large numbers using abbreviations:
  - 6,828 → 6.8k
  - 153,000 → 153k
  - This will make larger values easier to read and imply that the economy will scale to much bigger numbers.
- I'm not sure if the prices have been adjusted yet, but the economy needs more progression.
- I'm also seeing floating-point precision issues when adding money, for example 1531,880000000000005. This needs to be fixed so currency values are always clean and properly rounded.
Currencies & ATM
- Money and grass are currently confusing because both seem to be used as currencies.
- Should we bring the ATM back?
- If everything costs money, we need to make the distinction between money and grass much clearer.
- Ideally, they should have clearly different purposes and visual representations so players immediately understand that they are separate currencies.
Bag Capacity & Mowing
- Bag capacity definitely needs to increase significantly.
- I'm testing with a max-level mower, and the bag fills up every few seconds.
- Bag capacity should continue scaling incrementally with upgrades.
- Consider adding power-ups that temporarily increase bag capacity or improve collection efficiency.
- Make sure the player always has a way to unload their inventory and cannot get soft-locked.
Player Movement & Helpers
- If the player is not in a grass/mowable area, unmount them from the mower.
  - For example, when entering the egg den, the player shouldn't remain mounted.
- Helpers shouldn't be level-locked in the egg den.
- The egg den should become fully automated relatively quickly, allowing the player to focus on other activities.
Selling & Inventory Management
- Add a dedicated drop-off/sell area where players can unload items.
- Currently, the player can only unload items when they actively want to buy something, which is limiting.
- Players should always have somewhere to drop off and sell their collected items, independently of whether they're making a purchase.
Cow Barn & Production Balance
- The cow barn mechanics work, but production is accumulating much faster than demand.
- Scale customer orders based on available stock and production capacity.
- Avoid having all orders fulfilled all the time. There should be a healthy balance between supply and demand.
- Currently, I can end up with huge amounts of cheese sitting in stock. I'm already stuck with a lot of cheese.
- Increase the order queue capacity to around 5 trucks instead of capping it at 3.
- Increase the completion thresholds to approximately 98%.
  - The player will eventually have tractors and larger areas to manage, so the current thresholds may become too restrictive.
Cow Barn Customisation & Layout
- In the egg den, I can freely position stations and configure the conveyor belt.
- I can't do the same in the cow barn.
- Bring the same station positioning and conveyor belt setup functionality to the cow barn.
Selling Area & Item Placement
- The cheese drop-off point is currently too close to the selling area.
- Adjust the positioning to give the player more room and make the two areas easier to distinguish.
Treasures & Random Rewards
- Add more random treasures throughout the game.
- Introduce locked treasure chests as a new type of reward.
- Locked chests should:
  - Appear on the HUD when discovered.
  - Be accessible through a dedicated chest screen.
  - Offer the player the option to watch a video to unlock them.
  - Contain mostly money rewards for now.
- This should be implemented as a rewarded ad mechanic.
Overall Feedback
- Overall, the game is looking and feeling very satisfying.
- The core gameplay loop feels good, but progression, economy balancing, automation, and inventory management need further tuning.
- The biggest priorities are:
  1. Significantly increase bag capacity and upgrade progression.
  2. Improve economy scaling and currency clarity.
  3. Balance production against customer demand, especially in the cow barn.
  4. Ensure players always have a way to unload inventory and cannot get soft-locked.
  5. Expand automation and customisation options.
  6. Add more treasures and rewarded-ad chest opportunities.
